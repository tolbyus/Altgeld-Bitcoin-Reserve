# Reserve, Governance & Community Distribution Framework

**Altgeld Bitcoin Reserve / ABR Wealth Fund DAO LLC**  
**Framework document - August 2026**

A long-term Community Bitcoin Reserve model for Altgeld Gardens, structured for adaptation by other communities and municipalities.

> ABR could be the most significant Black economic experiment of the digital age. Communities deserve to grow with America, not behind it. ABR lets a neighborhood invest side by side with the country - and own a piece of the scarcest asset ever created.

## 1. Purpose

ABR is designed to create a lasting Bitcoin asset for Altgeld Gardens while establishing a model that can later be adapted by other communities and municipalities.

Communities should have an opportunity to build long-term ownership rather than always starting from zero.

## 2. Structure and Roles

**ABR Wealth Fund DAO LLC** is the Reserve and governance entity. It holds and protects Reserve Bitcoin, manages multisignature custody, maintains proof-of-reserves, coordinates governance, approves releases, and protects the 21 BTC objective.

**ABR Foundation** is the community, education, and public-benefit arm. Its work may include Bitcoin education, financial literacy, youth programming, digital literacy, community workshops, community events, community engagement, and future community-development initiatives.

The Foundation helps connect the Reserve to real community needs but does not independently control Reserve Bitcoin.

## 3. The Governance Body

"Governance body" means the governing members of ABR Wealth Fund DAO LLC, acting under the Reserve Policy. It is the only body that can approve a release of Reserve Bitcoin.

It is distinct from the three keyholders, who execute approved transactions, and from the ABR Foundation board, which sets program priorities but cannot authorize movement of Reserve Bitcoin. A person may serve in more than one role only where the governing documents expressly permit it.

## 4. Pathway to 21 Bitcoin

ABR can build toward 21 BTC over time through lawful pathways including Bitcoin contributions, private-sector partnerships, corporate sponsorships, philanthropy, individual donors, community fundraising, business-generated revenue, public-private partnerships, government participation where legally authorized, institutional contributions, mining partnerships, grants where permitted, and other approved strategies.

## 5. The Five-Year Hold

Reserve Bitcoin is held for an initial five years before community releases begin. Bitcoin's issuance schedule includes a halving roughly every four years; a five-year hold gives the Reserve time to remain invested across at least one full halving interval and reduces pressure to react to short-term volatility.

### Commencement

The five-year hold runs from the date the Reserve Policy is formally adopted by the governance body and applies to the Reserve as a whole. Bitcoin contributed after that date does not restart or extend the clock.

### Permitted Use During the Hold

Reserve Bitcoin is not intended for ordinary spending, payroll, personal expenses, loans, collateral, leverage, or speculative trading.

> Build the asset first. Give it time to mature. Then create a disciplined pathway for it to help the community without draining the Reserve.

## 6. Annual Release Ceiling

After the five-year hold, ABR may release up to 0.21 BTC per year for approved community programs and future infrastructure. The amount is a maximum, not a requirement; governance may release less or 0 BTC in a given year, and unused capacity does not accumulate into future years.

If ABR reaches 21 BTC, 0.21 BTC equals 1% of the original 21 BTC target.

## 7. Custody and Multisignature Governance

Reserve Bitcoin is held in 2-of-3 multisignature custody. Two of three keys are required to move Bitcoin, but key possession alone does not authorize spending. Governance approval must come first.

### Exhibit 2 - Keyholder Roles

1. **ABR Keyholder** - protects the Reserve, mission, governance, and institutional continuity.
2. **Community Keyholder** - represents resident priorities and protects one Reserve security key. Selected through an open community process and serving a defined, renewable term.
3. **Public Accountability Keyholder** - a government official, designated government or public-institution representative, or approved public-accountability representative who adds transparency, security, and accountability.

**Governance approval + multisignature execution = valid Reserve release.**

The Public Accountability role should attach to a defined seat rather than to one politician, so responsibility transfers when leadership changes. A 3-of-5 multisignature option may be used where a community, municipality, institution, or partner wants additional expertise or more distributed governance.

## 8. Key Succession and Continuity

A keyholder seat may become vacant through resignation, removal, incapacity, death, or departure from the underlying office. The governance body fills a vacant seat within a defined replacement window and, on doing so, migrates the Reserve to a newly generated multisignature quorum so that no retired key remains capable of signing.

Key material is backed up and geographically separated; recovery instructions are held under seal. Keyholders confirm control of their key at least annually, and each confirmation is noted in public reporting.

Because only two of three signatures are required, a single unavailable or non-cooperative keyholder cannot freeze an approved release - and no single keyholder can ever move Bitcoin alone.

## 9. Community Governance and Annual Roundtable

Throughout the year, the Community Representative/Keyholder and ABR Foundation gather needs, ideas, and priorities from residents. Once per year, ABR holds an Annual Community Reserve Roundtable in the neighborhood, a library, City Hall, a municipal or government building, or another trusted public location.

The governance body reviews the options, protects the Reserve Policy, addresses conflicts, and approves or rejects any proposed release. Where a release is approved over the Community Keyholder's stated objection, that objection is recorded in the public report.

ABR will maintain proof-of-reserves and publicly report holdings, acquisitions, approved releases, use of funds, remaining Bitcoin, and community results, alongside a continuous public display of Bitcoin price and Reserve position.

## 11. Summary of Safeguards

| Risk addressed | Structural safeguard |
|---|---|
| Control by one person | 2-of-3 multisignature custody; no unilateral movement |
| Spending without authority | Governance approval required before execution |
| Short-term reaction to volatility | Five-year initial hold |
| Depletion of principal | 0.21 BTC annual ceiling; no accumulation of unused capacity |
| Loss or departure of a keyholder | Succession protocol and quorum migration |
| Political turnover | Public Accountability role attached to a seat, not a person |
| Opacity | Proof-of-reserves and continuous public reporting |

## 12. Risk Considerations

Bitcoin is volatile. The value of the Reserve may decline substantially, including for extended periods, and past performance does not indicate future results. The five-year hold, the 0.21 BTC annual ceiling, and multisignature custody are designed to make that volatility survivable - not to eliminate it.

ABR does not promise returns, distributions, or a fixed dollar value to any resident, partner, or public body.

## 13. Defined Terms

### Reserve Bitcoin

Bitcoin held by ABR Wealth Fund DAO LLC under the Reserve Policy, subject to the hold and release limits described here.

### Reserve Policy

The governing policy adopted by the governance body that controls custody, approval, release, and reporting of Reserve Bitcoin.

### Release

A transfer of Reserve Bitcoin approved by the governance body after the five-year hold, within the annual ceiling.

### Keyholder

A person or seat holding one signing key in the multisignature quorum. Keyholders execute approved transactions; they do not authorize them.
