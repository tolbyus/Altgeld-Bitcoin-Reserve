## HR0446 - Adopted Recognition

The Illinois House adopted HR0446 on October 14, 2025. The resolution recognizes the Altgeld Bitcoin Reserve as the nation's first community-run Bitcoin reserve and commends Altgeld Gardens for leadership, innovation, and dedication to financial empowerment.

HR0446 is a resolution of recognition. It does not appropriate money, acquire Bitcoin, enact the private ABR framework into law, or itself establish a State program.

## SB3743 and HB5621 - Proposed Community Bitcoin Reserve Act

SB3743 and HB5621 are companion-style proposals to create a Community Bitcoin Reserve Program within the Illinois Department of Commerce and Economic Opportunity and designate Altgeld Bitcoin Reserve as the first community reserve site.

As introduced, the proposals include provisions concerning:

- Budget-neutral State acquisition authority, subject to General Assembly authorization
- Multisignature cold-storage custody
- A community-designated keyholder
- Proof-of-reserve reports and independent audits
- ABR Wealth Fund DAO LLC as an administrative and fiduciary agent, not a State entity
- ABR Foundation administration of authorized community programs without custody of the Bitcoin
- A minimum five-year hold from the date of acquisition
- A maximum annual program release of 0.21 BTC that also may not exceed 1% of total Bitcoin held at the time of release
- Restrictions on trading, lending, leverage, collateral, derivatives, and yield-generating activity

## Important Distinction

The private August 2026 ABR framework and the proposed State legislation are related but are not identical legal instruments.

For example, the ABR framework describes a five-year hold beginning when the Reserve Policy is formally adopted and applying to the Reserve as a whole. The introduced bills describe a minimum five-year hold from the date of acquisition for Bitcoin held under the proposed Act.

If either bill is amended or enacted, the official Illinois General Assembly record controls for the State program. The governing documents of ABR Wealth Fund DAO LLC and ABR Foundation control the private ABR structure unless applicable law provides otherwise.
