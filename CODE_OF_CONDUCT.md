# ABR Community Code of Conduct

The Altgeld Bitcoin Reserve (ABR) repository is intended to support serious public-interest collaboration across Bitcoin development, community work, policy, education, governance, security, and civic technology.

## Expected Conduct

Contributors should:

- Treat residents, contributors, reviewers, public officials, and partner organizations with respect.
- Critique ideas, code, documents, and assumptions rather than attacking people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence, reasoning, and proposed alternatives when possible.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or targeted intimidation.
- Respect privacy and operational-security boundaries.
- Keep public discussion focused on the project and its public-interest purpose.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when they could materially affect a contribution or recommendation.
- Avoid presenting personal opinions as adopted ABR policy.

## Security and Privacy Boundaries

Do not publish or request:

- Seed phrases or private keys
- Recovery material
- Hardware-wallet PINs
- Signing credentials
- Physical custody locations
- Private personal information
- Sensitive internal procedures that could increase custody risk

Security vulnerabilities that could create real risk should be reported privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Good-Faith Technical Disagreement

Bitcoin, public policy, governance, and security can involve strong disagreements. That is acceptable.

A productive disagreement should focus on questions such as:

- Is the claim accurate?
- Can it be independently verified?
- What assumptions does the design rely on?
- What security or governance tradeoffs exist?
- Does the proposal increase or reduce public transparency?
- Can a resident understand what the system is actually doing?

## Unacceptable Conduct

Examples include:

- Harassment or repeated personal attacks
- Publishing private information without permission
- Threats or encouragement of violence
- Deliberately misleading contributors about Reserve holdings or governance status
- Attempts to obtain custody secrets
- Spam, scams, token promotion, or unrelated investment solicitation
- Impersonating ABR representatives, government officials, or contributors

## Project Moderation

Maintainers may edit, hide, close, lock, or remove contributions that violate these standards or create security, privacy, legal, or operational risk.

Repeated or serious violations may result in participation restrictions.

## Scope

This code applies to participation in this repository and project-controlled collaboration spaces. It does not convert every disagreement involving ABR into a moderation matter.
