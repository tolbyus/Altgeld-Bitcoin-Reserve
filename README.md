# Altgeld Bitcoin Reserve

**A Bitcoin-only Community Reserve framework for Altgeld Gardens, Chicago.**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **ABR does not currently hold 21 BTC.** The 21 BTC figure is the Reserve's long-term goal, not its present balance. Current holdings must be confirmed through ABR's public proof-of-reserve interface and dated third-party disclosures.

The Altgeld Bitcoin Reserve (ABR) is designed to create a lasting Bitcoin asset for Altgeld Gardens while establishing a transparent model that may later be adapted by other communities and municipalities.

> ABR could be the most significant Black economic experiment of the digital age. Communities deserve to grow with America, not behind it. ABR lets a neighborhood invest side by side with the country - and own a piece of the scarcest asset ever created.

## Purpose

ABR is designed to give Altgeld Gardens an entry point into long-term Bitcoin ownership while connecting the Reserve to education, transparency, community participation, and future infrastructure.

The Reserve is not presented as a promise of profit or a guaranteed distribution. It is a disciplined framework for acquiring, securing, verifying, and carefully governing Bitcoin for long-term community benefit.

## Structure and Roles

### ABR Wealth Fund DAO LLC

The Reserve and governance entity. Under the framework, it holds and protects Reserve Bitcoin, manages multisignature custody, maintains proof-of-reserves, coordinates governance, approves releases, and protects the 21 BTC objective.

### ABR Foundation

The community, education, and public-benefit arm. Its work may include Bitcoin education, financial literacy, youth programming, digital literacy, community workshops, community events, community engagement, and future community-development initiatives.

The Foundation helps connect the Reserve to community needs but does not independently control Reserve Bitcoin.

## Reserve Lifecycle

**Acquire -> Secure -> Verify -> Hold -> Carefully Release -> Reinvest**

ABR may build toward the 21 BTC goal over time through lawful pathways including contributions, private-sector partnerships, corporate sponsorships, philanthropy, individual donors, community fundraising, business-generated revenue, public-private partnerships, legally authorized government participation, institutional contributions, mining partnerships, permitted grants, and other approved strategies.

## Five-Year Hold and Annual Release Ceiling

The framework provides that:

- The five-year hold begins when the Reserve Policy is formally adopted by the governance body.
- The hold applies to the Reserve as a whole. Later contributions do not restart or extend the clock.
- During the hold, Reserve Bitcoin is not intended for ordinary spending, payroll, personal expenses, loans, collateral, leverage, or speculative trading.
- After the hold, governance may approve a release of up to 0.21 BTC in a year for approved community programs and future infrastructure.
- A release is permitted, not required. Governance may approve less than 0.21 BTC or no release at all.
- Unused release capacity does not accumulate into later years.
- If ABR eventually reaches its 21 BTC goal, 0.21 BTC would equal 1% of that original target.

## Governance and Custody

The governance body authorizes Reserve activity. Keyholders execute an approved transaction but do not independently authorize spending.

The standard framework uses three keyholder seats:

1. **ABR Keyholder** - protects the Reserve, mission, governance, and institutional continuity.
2. **Community Keyholder** - represents resident priorities and protects one Reserve security key through a defined, renewable community process.
3. **Public Accountability Keyholder** - a government, public-institution, or approved accountability representative who adds transparency, security, and continuity.

Two of three keys are required to move Bitcoin. No single keyholder can move Reserve Bitcoin alone. An expanded 3-of-5 structure may be used when additional expertise or more distributed governance is appropriate.

**Governance approval + multisignature execution = valid Reserve release.**

## Community Process

ABR's framework calls for an Annual Community Reserve Roundtable in a trusted public location. Throughout the year, the Community Representative/Keyholder and ABR Foundation gather needs, ideas, and resident priorities.

The release sequence is:

**Community need -> Roundtable -> Governance approval -> Multisignature execution -> Public reporting**

## Transparency

ABR intends to publicly report:

- Current Reserve holdings
- Acquisitions and milestones
- Approved releases
- Use of released funds
- Remaining Bitcoin
- Community results
- Current Bitcoin price and Reserve value

The public record must always distinguish the **21 BTC goal** from the **actual BTC held**.
